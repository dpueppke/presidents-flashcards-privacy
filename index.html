Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

US Presidents Study Cards is designed as a simple study and reference app. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, location data, contacts, photos, browsing history, or other personal information.

The app may offer a one-time in-app purchase to unlock all content. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

The app may store preferences on your device, such as appearance settings, card order, study mode, haptics, and unlock status. This information stays on your device and is used only to make the app work as expected.

Analytics and Tracking

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or cross-app tracking.

Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for students and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

External Links

The app may include source and credit links for historical information or public-domain images. Those websites are operated by third parties and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy
